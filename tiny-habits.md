# Tiny Habits

## Question 1: Most interesting story or idea (Video 1)

The idea I found most interesting is that you should start with a very tiny habit. If a habit is small, you don't need much motivation, so you can do it even on a bad day. Once it becomes routine, it grows naturally.

## Question 2: How can B = MAP make new habits easier?

B = MAP means Behavior happens when Motivation, Ability and a Prompt come together at the same moment.

- **M (Motivation):** how much you want to do the habit.
- **A (Ability):** how easy the habit is to do. Make it tiny so it's easy.
- **P (Prompt):** the reminder that tells you to do it now, like "after I brush my teeth."

To build a habit more easily, make it tiny, attach it to something you already do, and don't rely on motivation alone.

## Question 3: Why is it important to "Shine" or celebrate?

Celebrating gives you a good feeling right after the habit. The brain connects that feeling to the habit and wants to repeat it.

## Question 6: How to make a habit easier to do

Follow the 4 laws:

- **Make it obvious:** put cues where you can see them.
- **Make it attractive:** pair it with something you enjoy.
- **Make it easy:** start with 2 minutes and reduce the steps.
- **Make it satisfying:** reward yourself right away and track your progress.

## Question 7: How to make a habit harder to do

Do the opposite of the 4 laws:

- **Make it invisible:** remove the cues.
- **Make it unattractive:** think about the harm it causes.
- **Make it difficult:** add steps between you and the habit.
- **Make it unsatisfying:** add a cost, like telling a friend or losing a reward.

## Question 8: A habit I want to do more

**Habit: reading daily**

- **Obvious:** keep the book on my pillow.
- **Attractive:** read with a cup of tea I love.
- **Easy:** start with just 1 page a day.
- **Satisfying:** tick a calendar box and celebrate with "I'm a reader!"

## Question 9: A habit I want to do less

**Habit: scrolling on my phone too much**

- **Invisible:** keep the phone in another room at night and turn off notifications.
- **Unattractive:** remind myself it wastes time and makes me tired.
- **Hard:** log out of apps and set a 15-minute app limit.
- **Unsatisfying:** tell a friend my goal so I feel accountable.
