# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment is any unwelcome verbal, visual or physical conduct of a sexual nature that creates a hostile work environment. The impact on the person matters, not the intent.

- **Verbal:** sexual jokes, comments on clothing or body, innuendos, repeated date requests after a "no", rumours about someone's personal life.
- **Visual:** sexual posters, cartoons, screen savers, emails or texts.
- **Physical:** unwanted touching or hugging, blocking movement, leering or staring.
- **Quid pro quo:** a supervisor offering rewards or threatening penalties for sexual favours.
- **Gender discrimination:** stereotyping, or giving extra work or fewer opportunities because of gender.

## 2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

1. Tell the person clearly that the behaviour is unwelcome and ask them to stop.
2. Keep a record: date, time, place, witnesses and what happened.
3. If it continues, report it in writing to a member of the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
4. If the incident is serious, file a police complaint.

Witnesses should report too, since anyone with knowledge of the incident can lodge a complaint.
