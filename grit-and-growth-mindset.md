# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1

**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.**

Grit means having the passion and patience to chase goals that take years to achieve. It is about staying committed every single day, not for a few weeks, but for the long haul, and treating life as a long-distance race rather than a quick dash.

## Question 2

**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.**

The video talks about two kinds of mindsets:

1. **Growth Mindset:** These people feel that abilities can be developed through practice, so they trust that they can keep learning and improving.
2. **Fixed Mindset:** These people feel that abilities are something you are born with, so they think learning and improving make little difference.

## Question 3

**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

**Internal Locus of Control** is the belief that you are in charge of your own life, and that your success depends on the effort you put in.

**Main Points of the Video:**
* **Effort makes a difference:** When you see that your actions change the results, it becomes easier to stay motivated.
* **Avoid making excuses:** Blaming luck or thinking "I am not smart enough" only leads to giving up.
* **How to practice it:** Solve a small problem in your life and remind yourself, "I achieved this through my own hard work."
