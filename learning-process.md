# Learning Process

## Question 1
**What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**

The Feynman Technique is a learning method that requires you to teach a concept in simple terms, as if explaining it to a child.

## Question 2
**In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?**

The most interesting idea for me was the use of learning modes, i.e. focus and diffused, and overcoming procrastination using the Pomodoro Technique.

## Question 3
**What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

- **Active Mode:** Concentrated, detail-oriented thinking for familiar ideas.
- **Diffuse Mode:** Relaxed, wide-ranging thinking helpful for grasping new concepts.

---

## Question 4
**According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**

1. Deconstruct the skill into smaller parts.
2. Learn enough to self-correct.
3. Remove barriers to practice.
4. Practice for at least 20 hours.

2. Implement focused and diffused learning modes.
3. Overcome procrastination by dividing work into smaller tasks to improve my attention.
