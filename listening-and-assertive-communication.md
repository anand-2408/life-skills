# Listening and Assertive Communication

## **Question 1**
**What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?**

- Focus on the Speaker: Pay full attention to the speaker and the topic without getting distracted by your own thoughts.
- Avoid Interrupting: Let the other person finish speaking before responding.
- Use Door Openers: Use phrases that show interest and encourage the speaker to continue talking.
- Show Interest Through Body Language: Use appropriate body language and take notes during important conversations.
- Paraphrase and Clarify: Repeat what the speaker has said in your own words to ensure that you both understand each other correctly.

## **Question 2**
**According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)**

Reflective listening is a way of listening carefully to someone and repeating their ideas in your own words to make sure you have understood them correctly.
